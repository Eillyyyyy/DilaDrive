## 1. Live schema

`pg_dump --schema-only --no-owner --no-privileges --schema=public` succeeded. PostgreSQL confirmed `transaction_read_only=on`. The dump was held in memory. The catalog register below cross-checks the exact expressions, foreign-key actions and constraint-backed indexes. Primary keys and ordinary non-partial, non-unique indexes are omitted from that register. No services were started and no database writes were attempted. Source tracing establishes implementation and intended clients. End-to-end execution and deployment parity are **UNVERIFIED**.
